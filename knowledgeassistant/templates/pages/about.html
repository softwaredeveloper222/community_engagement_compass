About the Compass

A fairytale companion for community engagement — gathering trusted frameworks into one enchanted library you can ask, search, and learn from.

✦   ✧   ✦

The Story

The Community Engagement Compass is a proof-of-concept assistant designed to support healthcare and public health professionals applying trusted community engagement frameworks. Upload your tomes — the assistant reads, remembers, and answers with citations from the source.

How it works

  1. Upload PDFs into your enchanted library.
  2. The compass extracts text, splits it into pages, and generates embeddings.
  3. Ask any question — semantic search finds the most relevant passages.
  4. The assistant composes an answer and cites every source.

Trust & safety

  • Answers are grounded in your uploaded documents.
  • Citations are visible on every response.
  • Your library is private to your account.
  • Feedback is welcomed — help us refine the magic.
